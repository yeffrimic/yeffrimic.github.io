Travel log · #EmprendeTour

Why this trip?

First, to prove to ourselves that it can be done, and that you don't have to be that crazy to pull it off… or do you?

The destination: Costa Rica, for Global Entrepreneurship Week and to prepare the first public policy hackathon created by and for young Costa Rican entrepreneurs. Every kilometer brings us closer to turning ideas into real proposals.

Photos and video from the road

We document the route with a drone and our phone cameras.

Tech for small businesses

We help small entrepreneurs build their website or simple systems to digitize and transform how they work. Sometimes it's as simple as having their own site with their own domain.

Videos and podcasts

We record videos and podcasts with entrepreneurs and create content together with them.

Talks along the way

We give talks at institutions and universities in the countries we ride through.

The goal is to build momentum for Hazlo Ahora ("Do It Now"), an organization that sets out to inspire people, and for yeffrimic, which has the same goal. I'm also part of Hazlo Ahora.
